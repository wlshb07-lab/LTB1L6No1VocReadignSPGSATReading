🌟 什麼是「訊號詞」(Signal Words)？

就像我們在路上開車需要看紅綠燈跟路標一樣，文章裡也有專屬的「路標」，我們稱它為「訊號詞」。只要看到它們，大腦就會自動切換模式，告訴你：「嘿！接下來要講時間順序囉！」或是「注意！這裡是因果關係喔！」
類別 (Category)定義 (Definition)常見訊號詞 (Common Signal Words)
時間順序 (Time Order)表示事件先後、時間點的詞when, before, after, over time, today, first, then
空間順序 (Spatial Order)表示位置、方位的詞in, on, at, in front of, on top of, left/right hand
因果關係 (Cause & Effect)表示原因、結果、目的的詞because, so, since, as a result, so that, this is why

✍️ 句型一：It is/was...that....（分裂句 / 強調句）

語境漫畫說明：
April: Johnny, you want fried rice, right?
Alan: No. He wants beef noodles.
Johnny: No, it is dumplings that I want!

在此語境中，爸媽皆誤會小孩想吃的東西是什麼，小孩需要特別強調他想吃的是「水餃」，而不是「炒飯」或「牛肉麵」，因此使用強調句，強調某個未知的或被誤解的訊息。
1. 此句型主要功能是用來強調某個訊息，因此稱為強調句。在此句型中，將想要強調的訊息置於It is/was與that之間，句子其餘字詞則置於that之後，因此又稱為分裂句。
• Johnny wants dumplings. → It is dumplings that Johnny wants. (not fried rice or beef noodles)
2. 強調句中，被強調的訊息可以是人、物、地點、時間、原因等。
• It is Ella that is going to get married in October.
• It was in this comic book store that I first met my wife.
• It was after I graduated from junior high school that I moved to Canada.
Example (課文)：It was on this day that the United States became independent. (line 19)

✍️ 句型二：(1) so + adj./adv. + that + S + V　(2) such (+ a/an) + adj. + N + that + S + V 如此……以致於……

語境漫畫說明：
Jack: I am so hungry that I could eat a horse.
The horse: Neigh...

在此語境中，旅人相當飢餓，到了幾乎可以吃下一匹馬的程度，因此使用帶有因果語意的「so...that...」句型。
Examples (課文)：
1. Over time, this outer layer has been exposed to the air and oxidized so much that it has turned green. (line 15)
2. It is such a popular landmark that every visitor to New York City has heard of it and wants to pay a visit to Liberty Island. (line 35)

此句型在英語實際溝通中具有「強調」的功能，強化了前後兩個子句之間的「因果關係」。而 so 後面所接的形容詞或副詞也同時增加強度，such 後面所接的名詞意涵也得以凸顯。

May is so beautiful that I can't take my eyes off her.
It was such a lovely day that nobody wanted to stay at home.

🗽 補充練習 B：中翻英（so/such...that....）

1. 這是個如此好的機會，你不應該錯過（miss out on）。
2. 這些小孩太吵了，以致於我無法專注在功課上。
3. 這場車禍太嚴重，以致於那名駕駛當場死亡（die on the spot）。

5. 雨下得如此大以致於Billy全身都溼透了。
參考答案：
1. This is such a great opportunity that you should not miss out on it. / This opportunity is so great that you should not miss out on it.
2. The kids are so noisy that I can't concentrate on my homework.
3. The car accident was so serious that the driver died on the spot. / This was such a serious car accident that the driver died on the spot.
4. Kim woke/got up so early that she could see the sunrise.
5. It rained so heavily that Billy got soaking wet. / It was such a heavy rain that Billy got soaking wet.
